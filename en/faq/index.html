Questions

Answers to common questions about the OrroFi app, Mi gestor and Mi red.

Frequently asked questions

  1. Who can use OrroFi?

    Subscribers of our operator on the Costa Brava. If you are not a customer yet, you can check coverage for an address in the app and leave a request.

  2. Is Mi gestor a person?

    No. It is an assistant with artificial intelligence, and it says so in the first message. If you need a person, tap “Talk to a person”.

  3. When do staff reply?

    During working hours. If no one is there now, the app says so: leave your request, and we will get in touch.

  4. Do I need to understand the technology?

    No. For Mi red you will need the password on the sticker on the router. The app explains the rest, step by step.

  5. Which router does Mi red need?

    The one our technician installs.

  6. Can I add the family's phones?

    Yes: with a QR code when you meet, or by checking six digits over a video call. The owner can remove a phone that was added.

  7. How do I change the time of a technician's visit?

    Ask for another time through Mi gestor. A member of staff will confirm the new date.

  8. How are bills paid?

    From a bank account, by SEPA. A member of staff can help you change the payment method.

  9. Can I get a copy of my data or delete the account?

    Yes, in the “Privacy” section. The account is deleted after the contracts end.

  10. Which languages is the app in?

    Spanish and English.

  11. When will the app be released?

    We are preparing the release on the App Store for iPhone and will announce the date. The Android version comes later.

  12. Why does the site use made-up data?

    These are screens of the coming version: the names, addresses and amounts on them are invented, and the prices are an example.
